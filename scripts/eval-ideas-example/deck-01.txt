Example Co — Spring Launch — Pitch Round 1

Slide 1: The brief
Example Co wants night-shift workers to think of its coffee as the drink that keeps them going.

Slide 2: Idea 1 — Night Owl Route
A late-night radio takeover between midnight and 5 am.
An owl mascot hosts the show and reads out listeners' shift stories.
Tone: warm and tongue in cheek.

Slide 3: Idea 2 — Kitchen Table Stories
Families film short clips at the kitchen table at the end of a night shift.
Social-first: vertical video, posted by the families themselves.
